My Favorite Scripture

My favorite scripture is from a very famous verse in John 15:18-19, where it says: "If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before it hated you. If ye were of the world, the world would love his own: but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hateth you." This whole chapter is about when Jesus chose His servants and they are ordained by Him. The whole chapter talks about how Jesus is our vine and we are the branches, and that we are all connected to Him spiritually.

After that, this scripture has always been constantly reminding me that the world hates and fights true religion, "but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world." Personally, back in high school I was still trying to strengthen my faith in our Heavenly Father when surrounded by people of different faiths. I have to constantly remind myself that I don't have to argue about my faith when friends or people ask about it, and my best approach is to share my testimony about the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. During that time I did my personal study on how to share my testimony with my friend about the true church.

To conclude, as a convert I have always been grateful to our Heavenly Father for leading me to a path of where I am now. There are times when I am spiritually challenged, but this verse is always my go-to scripture if I ever feel drifted away from Heavenly Father. Lastly, I would like to share my favorite talk from General Conference by Elder Neil L. Andersen, called "Beware of the Evil behind the Smiling Eyes." The gift of the Holy Ghost is a spiritual gift. It is sensitive and will not be associated with unworthiness. You cannot offend or ignore it one day and expect it to strengthen you the next day. But as you heed its promptings and remain righteous, it will grow stronger within you. The Holy Ghost warned my friend of physical danger, and the Holy Ghost will also warn you of spiritual danger.
